第一张里，孩子还伏在老人背上；往后，孩子坐在推车里，被大人牵着，然后跑到了前面。大人始终在一臂之内：弯腰去拉孩子，跟在身后，骑车陪在旁边。到最后，姐姐推着弟弟穿过机场，一群背粉色书包的女孩结伴走远，只有一个大人走在她们中间。

> In the first frame a child still rides on a grandmother's back. After that, children are pushed in strollers, led by the hand, and then run ahead. The adults stay within arm's reach: bending to catch a child, following on foot, cycling alongside. By the end, a girl pushes her little brother through an airport, and a group of girls with pink backpacks walks off together, one adult among them.

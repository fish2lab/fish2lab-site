春天的傍晚，一群学生在玉兰树下手拉着手，排成长长的一列。这组合影里，人们总被手中的某样东西聚到一起：一只毛绒玩偶，一个纸板头套，一束花，一件学位服，或是身旁那个人的手。从街头、同好聚会、台阶前，到家里的沙发，镜头留下了他们恰好站在一起的片刻。

> On a spring evening, students join hands in a long line beneath magnolia trees. Across these group photos, people are gathered by something they hold: a plush doll, a cardboard mask, a bouquet, a graduation gown, or the hand of the person beside them. From a shopping street and fan meetups to a building's front steps and a family sofa, the camera keeps the moment they happened to stand together.

春节夜里河边点燃的烟花，三月雪夜球场上越滚越大的雪球，四月雨中的小路，八月小区广场上追跑的孩子。天黑以后，路灯、窗户和彩灯接管了光线，人却没有散去。这组照片按季节排开，拍的是入夜之后还留在户外的人。

> From a firework lit on the Spring Festival night, to a snowball rolled across a floodlit field in March, a rainy path in April, and children running through a residential square in August. After dark, streetlamps, windows and coloured strings take over the light, yet people stay outside. Arranged by season, these frames follow the ones who have not gone home.

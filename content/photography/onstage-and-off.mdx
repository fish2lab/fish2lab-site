工人在草坪上拼起钢架，把一排排灯挂上天空；傍晚的校园里，乐队接好音箱，吉他手朝人群扬起手臂。这组照片从台前和台下两边看各种活动：弹琴的人，吹口琴的人，相拥的扮装者，展会上挥手的来宾，也有低头记笔记、举起手机的观众。每一场活动都靠他们一起撑起来。

> Workers bolt steel frames together on a lawn and hang rows of lights against the sky; at dusk on campus, a band plugs in and the guitarist throws an arm toward the crowd. These photos follow events from both sides of the stage: a pianist, a harmonica ensemble, costumed players embracing, a guest waving at an expo, and an audience taking notes or raising phones. Every event is held up by all of them.

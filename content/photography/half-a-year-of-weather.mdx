从三月的雪到八月的雷雨，这组照片跟着半年的天气走。雪落在校园的林荫道和山村的屋顶上，雾霾罩住成片的大棚，雨后傍晚有两个人撑伞走过草地，列车窗外，积云和风车立在望不到头的田野上。每一张里的树、山和云旁边，都有电线、玻璃或房子。

> From March snow to August storms, these photographs follow half a year of weather. Snow settles on a campus avenue and on the roofs of a mountain village, haze covers rows of greenhouses, two people cross a lawn under umbrellas on a wet evening, and outside a train window, cumulus clouds and wind turbines stand over endless fields. In every frame, the trees, hills and clouds share space with wires, glass or houses.

午后，两名排球队员端着饭碗，沿林荫道小跑回场；空地上一只排球被抛向天空，一个路人从画面前走过。随后天色转紫，操场的灯亮起来，散步的人绕着跑道走，篮球场上有人运球突破。最后一张，戴眼镜的女孩举拍踮脚，笑着去够头顶的那一球。

> In the afternoon, two volleyball players jog back along a tree-lined road carrying bowls of food; on an open square a ball goes up while a passer-by walks through the frame. Then the sky turns purple, the floodlights come on, people stroll around the track, and someone drives to the basket. In the last frame a girl in glasses stretches up with a racket, laughing, reaching for the shot over her head.

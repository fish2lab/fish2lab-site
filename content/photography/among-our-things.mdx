茶几上，灰猫端坐在果盘和茶杯之间，直直看着镜头；另一只虎斑猫趴在马桶盖上睡着。街边，橘猫蹲在垃圾桶顶，黑白猫在店门口回头。边牧在山路边嗅着草丛，又坐进敞开的后备箱。每只动物都占住了人的一样东西。

> A grey cat sits among the fruit plates on a coffee table and stares straight into the lens; a tabby sleeps on a toilet lid. On the street, a ginger stray crouches on a bin and a black-and-white cat looks back from a shop step. A border collie sniffs the verge of a mountain road, then sits in an open car boot. Each animal has claimed one of our things.
